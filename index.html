About Me

Where security meets
research & leadership.

I started my career as a Dual Student in IT Security at Boehringer Ingelheim, where over nearly five years I grew into an Information Protection & Security Officer and later a Student Coordinator — helping shape the next generation of IT talent.

Today I combine a freelance lecturing role at DHBW Ravensburg — teaching attack methods and defensive strategies — with a Master's in Information Systems and a concurrent B.Sc. in International Business & Economics at the University of Innsbruck. As 1st Deputy Chair of the Faculty Representation (SoWi), I advocate for over 4,000 students.
